Swisscom NEO is the initiative for the next generation of brand communication. The brief: a consistent visual system, maintained in Group Communications and built to last across every touchpoint.

## The starting point

Over time the visual expression had drifted between products, campaigns, and internal tools. Multiple sub-systems, multiple voices. The refresh had to bring the brand closer to its core — precise, calm, technical.

## The approach

We rebuilt the design system from the ground up: tokens as the source of truth, three weights instead of seven, a tighter image logic. Templates were maintained centrally and used decentrally — the relationship between consistency and speed flipped.

## The outcome

The NEO line is now consistently recognisable across web, app, out-of-home, and internal communications. Maintenance happens in a small, clear token structure. Marketing teams move faster because they have fewer decisions to make.

## What I take from it

- Design systems only work with clear ownership. Tokens without an owner decay.
- Reduction is a political decision. It only works when top management carries it.
- The most important debates were not about colour, but about responsibility.
