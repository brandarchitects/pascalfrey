In 2018 I founded Brand Architects — as an answer to a gap between the big agencies and the many freelancers. The aim: senior expertise at studio terms, with the discipline of a network.

## The starting point

Mid-sized Swiss companies face brand questions that are too small for full-service agencies and too strategic for freelancer pools. The gap was measurable. The solution had to stay lean.

## The approach

Brand Architects works as a studio network: a core team, complemented by selected specialists per project. No pitches, no pyramids — a senior practitioner is on every project.

The brand itself follows the same principle: restrained, precise, no agency posing. If you want to know what we can do, look at the work. Not the showreel.

## The outcome

Since 2018, Brand Architects has accompanied companies in tech, industry, and the public sector. Pipeline runs on referrals — not on outbound.

## What I take from it

- A brand for a brand consultancy is the hardest assignment. It's both self-portrait and promise.
- Staying small is a strategy, not a state. It has to be defended.
- Referral is the most honest marketing discipline. It can't be faked.
